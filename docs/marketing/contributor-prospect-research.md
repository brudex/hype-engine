# Contributor prospect research (private)

Prepared for Day 11 of the 30-day contributor-growth plan on 2026-08-06.

## Purpose and handling

This is an internal, source-linked research list for a future maintainer review.
It uses only public GitHub repository pages and public release credits. It does
not contain email addresses, location, employer details, activity tracking, or
information inferred from private sources. Appearance on this list is not an
endorsement, relationship, or permission to contact someone.

**No contact has been made.** Do not send a message, open an issue, comment,
mention, or publish any part of this list without explicit owner approval for
the individual destination and message. If outreach is later approved, verify
the source, the project's contribution rules, and that a specific Hype Engine
issue has been published first. Do not contact maintainers of adjacent products
solely because they maintain an adjacent product.

## Selection method

- Start with public repositories whose stated scope overlaps Hype Engine's
  verified work: self-hosted social scheduling, provider integrations, Docker,
  workflow automation, or visual flows.
- Include public contributors only where a repository or release credits a
  GitHub handle for relevant public work. The suggested Hype Engine draft is a
  topical match, not a claim about the person's availability or expertise.
- Include public project communities and directories as observation or future
  rule-review targets, not as places to post. Their rules and current activity
  must be rechecked before any approved outreach.
- Prefer the five published-ready *local* `good first issue` drafts before
  larger work. All Hype Engine issue drafts remain unpublished as of this
  research date.

## Prioritization for a later owner review

1. **First publish the prepared work.** The five `good first issue` and three
   `help wanted` drafts in `docs/marketing/contributor-issue-drafts.md` must be
   approved, labeled, and published before any invitation could provide a
   working destination.
2. **Use contribution fit, not project popularity.** A published issue should
   determine the small shortlist: GFI-01/GFI-02 for accessibility evidence,
   GFI-03/GFI-05 for provider-integration evidence, and HW-01/HW-02 for worker
   or flow evidence. Do not assume any person wants an invitation.
3. **Keep adjacent-project maintainers out of proactive outreach.** Entries
   1–3 and 11–20 are primarily research sources. They may help improve issue
   quality and comparisons, but are not a permission to recruit in another
   project's spaces.
4. **Revalidate immediately before drafting.** Confirm the exact public source,
   current contribution rules, and a concrete contribution issue. Personal
   outreach drafts belong to Day 12 and require owner approval before sending.

## Source verification

On 2026-08-06, the listed public GitHub sources were checked for the stated
project scope, contribution/community surface, or public release credits. The
research deliberately records stable repository URLs rather than copied
profiles, activity histories, or contact information. Current stars, issue
counts, availability, and community rules are intentionally not treated as
durable evidence and must be rechecked for any later decision.
