# Personal outreach drafts (private; do not send)

Prepared for Day 12 of the 30-day contributor-growth plan on 2026-08-07.

## Status and sending gate

These are **ten private drafts, not messages**. They deliberately contain no
email address, handle destination, tracking link, or request to reply. A
maintainer must not copy, adapt, or send one until all of the following are
true:

1. The linked Hype Engine issue has been owner-approved, labeled, and
   published from `docs/marketing/contributor-issue-drafts.md`; replace the
   `[published issue URL]` placeholder with that exact URL.
2. The public source in
   `docs/marketing/contributor-prospect-research.md` and the person's current
   contribution/contact preferences have been rechecked. Do not use a project
   issue tracker, discussion, forum, or community space as a recruiting
   channel.
3. The owner approves the named recipient, final text, and one permitted
   sending channel. A draft is not approval to send.
4. Send at most one respectful, low-pressure invitation. Do not follow up
   unless the person explicitly invites a follow-up; no response is a decline.

The source match below is a topical reason to consider a draft, not a claim
that the person is available, interested, qualified, or affiliated with Hype
Engine. The local issue IDs are not GitHub issue numbers and must never be
presented as published work.

## Maintainer review checklist

Before approving any one draft for a real message, confirm all of the
following for that draft:

- [ ] Its Hype Engine issue is published and still accurately scoped.
- [ ] The recipient's cited public work and current contact preference are
      still accurate.
- [ ] The chosen channel is explicitly permitted by the recipient and is not
      an adjacent project's support or community space.
- [ ] The final text names the published issue URL and adds no unsupported
      personal detail, performance claim, urgency, or incentive.
- [ ] The owner has approved that recipient, final wording, and channel.
- [ ] A single-send/no-follow-up record is kept without storing new personal
      data in this repository.
