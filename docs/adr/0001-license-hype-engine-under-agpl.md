# License Hype Engine under AGPL-3.0-or-later

Status: accepted

Date: 2026-07-27

## Context

Hype Engine is a self-hostable social-media management application whose
primary mode of use is through a network service. A permissive license would
allow a third party to modify the application, operate a proprietary hosted
competitor, and withhold those modifications from its users.

The project wants commercial use and community contributions to remain
possible while ensuring that improvements to hosted derivatives remain
available to their users.

## Decision

Hype Engine is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License version 3
or later (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`).

Contributors retain copyright in their contributions. Accepted contributions
are distributed under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.

The project will not require a Contributor License Agreement at this stage. A
CLA may be considered later only if commercial dual licensing becomes a
concrete requirement and after appropriate legal review.

## Consequences

- Anyone may use, modify, distribute, and commercially operate Hype Engine
  under the license terms.
- Operators of modified network versions must offer the corresponding source
  to users interacting with those versions.
- Covered derivatives must remain under the AGPL when conveyed.
- Some organizations may decline to adopt or contribute because of internal
  AGPL policies.
- Proprietary dual licensing of third-party contributions is not automatically
  available without additional permission from their copyright holders.
- The product interface should provide a prominent source-code link.
- Contribution documentation must clearly state that submitted contributions
  are accepted under `AGPL-3.0-or-later`.
